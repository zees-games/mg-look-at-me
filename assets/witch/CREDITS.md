# Witch

`witch.glb` is made from "Witch" by Tamko Dmitry, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/witch-0b6f69bbaa864bd78c0cfa98dabca0aa, licensed CC Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: the staff and bottles removed; the body made gaunt and hunched, with long crooked fingers, tattered hem and cloak and hollow eye sockets; the texture drained to a dark palette; the robe split into eight hanging panels in two tiers that swing and flare clear of her knees; jointed fingers on both hands; repainted at 4096 px with ray-traced ambient occlusion (`witch_ao.png`), a skin mask (`witch_sss.png`) and 2048 px copies for the web (`witch_web_*.png`); rigged and animated with idle, walk, attack_idle and attack clips (`tools/make_witch.py`).
