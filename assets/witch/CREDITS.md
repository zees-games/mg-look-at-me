# Witch

`witch.glb` is made from "Witch" by Tamko Dmitry, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/witch-0b6f69bbaa864bd78c0cfa98dabca0aa, licensed CC Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: the staff and bottles removed; the body made gaunt and hunched, with long crooked fingers, tattered hem and cloak and hollow eye sockets; the texture drained to a dark palette; rigged and animated with idle, walk, attack_idle and attack clips (`tools/make_witch.py`).
