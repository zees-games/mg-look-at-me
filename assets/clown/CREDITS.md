# Clown

`clown.glb` is made from "Da-Clown #StylizedBustChallenge" by Nwilly_art, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/da-clown-stylizedbustchallenge-59bdbb3cc60748b995106b6020b25303, licensed CC Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: the two eyeballs are separate meshes, the lower face is skinned to one jaw bone, scaled to metres and the textures re-encoded as JPEG (`tools/make_clown.py`).
